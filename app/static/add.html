Add a plug

The plug joins the Wi-Fi network this Pi hosts. Nothing here needs internet.

  1. 1

    Connect your phone to the plug

    Plug the new plug into a socket and give it about 30 seconds. It starts its own Wi-Fi network, named like ShellyPlugMG3-XXXXXX. The Pi looks for it, so you can join it by scanning a code instead of typing.

    Looking for the plug's Wi-Fi…

    Scan this code with your phone's camera and join the plug's network. If the phone says there is no internet, stay connected.

    Join it by hand instead

    Open your phone's Wi-Fi settings and choose the network that starts with Shelly. It has no password. If nothing shows up, hold the plug's button for about 10 seconds to reset it (see its manual), then wait for it to restart.
